Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

DesignLab processes information needed to provide accounts and learning features. This may include your email address, display name, learning progress, saved items, conversations with Design Mentor AI, and designs you choose to submit for analysis.

How information is used

  • Account information is used to authenticate you and maintain your profile.
  • Learning progress and saved items are used to provide platform features across sessions.
  • Content submitted to AI features is processed to generate the response you request.
  • Google sign-in, when configured and used, is handled by the platform's authentication provider.

Security and retention

Authentication and session management are handled by the platform provider. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in learning prompts. Information may be retained as needed to operate the service, comply with applicable obligations, and protect the platform.

Your choices

You can choose what content to upload and whether to use AI features. For account or privacy questions, contact the DesignLab site owner through the channel where you accessed the platform.
